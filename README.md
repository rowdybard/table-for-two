# Table for Two

A first-person restaurant game with three modes: **Solo date**, **Double date**, and **Blind date**. Take a seat, order dinner, talk naturally or type, and see how the evening unfolds.

Start with [CLAUDE.md](CLAUDE.md) for the project direction, architecture, provider rules and verification workflow.

Hosted game: [table-for-two.maxpug17.workers.dev](https://table-for-two.maxpug17.workers.dev). Physical gameplay and free Edge speech are enabled. Conversation defaults to the operator's Anthropic account; `/model horde` selects free community-hosted dialogue.

Solo date lets you choose Mira, Jules, or Dev, with a reactive waiter and neighboring diner. Double date brings two real friends and two independently selected AI dates to one shared table. Blind date is a solo evening with a surprise personality, tastes, and biography drawn from a stable random seed; its appearance and assigned character voice reuse the existing cast.

For Double date, one friend creates the table and shares the invite link or code. Each person chooses a different date and gets ready; the host starts. Movement and seating are independent. Both dates hear attributed public conversation, and sustained friend-only exchanges can make them feel excluded. Silence, menus, microphone denial and reconnection time do not count as neglect. Either friend can include the table again. The couples can have different farewells. After your own date ends, stay as a wingman: walk, talk to the remaining date, help with a table interruption or ask for water. You can open your own ending and return while your friend continues. Departed AI dates leave the scene and conversation targets; they do not hear later turns or acquire new memories.

Every mode draws one table story from eleven possibilities: feeling unwell, red wine on an outfit, an oversized order, flirting with the waiter, an awkward ex, an intrusive relative, an involuntary tic, an allergic reaction, too many drinks, a private conversation about attraction, or an unwelcome prejudiced comment. Each story has an opening choice and a follow-through choice, with character-specific reactions and factual callbacks in the notebook and conversation context. Double date shares one story at the table. Sensitive scenes preserve privacy and agency, medical scenes receive staff assistance, and kindness does not reverse a stated lack of romantic interest.

Each date also arrives with one of sixteen private wants for this evening: clear intentions, room to choose, an evening off from organizing, playful chemistry, comfortable disagreement, or another kind of connection. They give natural clues and answer candidly when asked. The on-demand notebook records only delivered remarks. The goal and an understanding score appear at the farewell, with supporting quotes from the actual evening. Noticing, understanding, and making room for the goal earn distinct milestones; repeated lines and flattery do not earn repeat points. Honest incompatibility can score fully. Earned understanding makes confidence possible; attraction, boundaries and each person's choices still decide whether romance is welcome.

Every preset and seeded Blind date starts highly skeptical. Their humor and delivery stay distinct, but charm, big claims, agreement, money and a friend vouching do not establish belief. The conversation policy asks for specific answers and actual follow-through, preserves claims as claims in memory, and challenges only contradictions in heard statements or recorded actions. It permits warmth before belief, revises doubt when evidence lands, and treats a fumbled correction or recognition error fairly. Noticing requires a previously heard clue; deeper understanding and respect require two distinct heard specifics and new player evidence. A positive social line, cleanup or apology alone cannot promote the date to confident comfort or unlock a romantic farewell. Boundary repair reopens conversation while lost confidence must be earned again. There is no additional provider request, background grading, or visible trust meter.

Goals are chosen on the server per evening; a Blind seed repeats its goal. Each Double date has independent progress, and a friend cannot earn the other player's understanding points. The existing completed-turn conversation response proposes bounded evidence, which the server checks against the current speaker's text and delivered clues. There are no additional model requests, background grading or speech calls. The additional context and assessment do add a small number of normal input/output tokens. Scenario and physical-action controllers do not grant agenda points automatically. Provider quality of the new rubric has not yet been benchmarked against live human play.

## A complete evening

Dinner now has a beginning, courses, shared activities, recoverable interruptions, and a farewell at the door. Each date has their own biography, tastes, vulnerabilities, conversation style, and closing lines. The intended pace is a 20–30 minute evening with conversation; you can linger or leave earlier.

- Your date chooses their own main. Try your food, check an incorrect order against the ticket, and either return it or keep the unexpected dish. The actual served meal changes with that choice.
- Offer your date a taste, choose a toast, ask for water, or check how they are feeling. Preferences can lead to a polite refusal; water takes time to arrive, and a request for a slower evening creates a quiet break. These are remembered actions, not repeatable affection points.
- Play three physical rounds of coaster curl with an independently controlled date. Drag to aim, or use the keyboard-accessible aim/power controls. Shared table games are optional.
- Add a freehand line to the date's napkin doodle and keep it. The drawing uses real pointer strokes and is never presented as having been recognized by a vision model.
- Wipe a spilled drink and steady the glass. Handle a noisy neighbor through the waiter or choose to focus on each other. Resolutions clear the visible disturbance and become part of the night's factual notebook.
- Choose dessert or skip it. Serve four portions onto two plates, settle the fictional restaurant bill, stand up, and walk to the entrance together. No real payment is taken.
- Choose a second-date invitation, friendship, or a goodbye. The date has an independent stance. Conversation and repairs can support another evening or a second chance; repeated boundary pressure can lead the date to leave. There is no numerical romance score.
- Download an illustrated memento and revisit completed evenings saved on this device. The local history stores endings, factual moments, napkin strokes and the final agenda reflection, including a few player quotes used as evidence. Microphone recordings and the full conversation transcript are not saved there.

Use the contextual table controls, or look at objects and press E. The plate offers a bite, the napkin can wipe a spill, and the glass can be put upright. The notebook separates conversation from memorable moments. Restaurant ambience and quiet lounge music can be toggled in Settings.

Ordering raises the table menu without hiding the restaurant; Escape puts it down. Table activities focus the existing board or lift the napkin/tray into view. An interruption puts down an active activity so its recovery controls remain available; reopen the activity afterward to continue the same round or drawing.

Stories and timed wrong-order, spill and noisy-neighbor incidents leave calm space between them. Moving to the next course does not manufacture an interruption, so a short evening need not encounter every event. Resolve an active table moment before progressing. New physical incidents stop once billing or farewell begins.

Scenario choices now change the evening itself: kitchen holds preserve remaining service time, bathroom/cleanup callbacks wait for the date to return physically, prepared extra dishes can be shared or boxed, uncooked extras can be canceled, and the itemized receipt follows those decisions. Ask Eli to resume through G after a held dinner; a resolved break also has a safe automatic fallback. A quiet-table choice relocates the dining station, seats, targets and collision body together. Medical Double Date exits reveal the affected date’s agenda and reflection while their friend’s evening can continue. Wrong plates arrive before eating; later spills and noisy-neighbor interruptions no longer require a previous incident to have occurred.

The seated table has finite water and bread, personal seasoning, adjustable cutlery, a foldable drawing napkin, a dimmable lamp, flowers and ceramic plates. E uses the aimed object; R or clicking a table prop leans in for a closer look with compact Use/Back controls. On phones, tap a prop to inspect it; a drag still looks around. Water levels, bread, seasoning and folded cloth update in the scene. Both friends share the basket, carafe, light and vase while retaining their own place settings. Empty supplies can be replenished through Eli's existing water service. Cosmetic table actions do not request dialogue, interrupt a response, change scores or enter conversation memory. Pausing, standing, active interruptions and farewells retain their authoritative action restrictions.

## Run locally

Requires Node 24+ and pnpm.

```powershell
pnpm install
Copy-Item .env.example .env
# Add ANTHROPIC_API_KEY to .env for live dialogue. Speech defaults to free Edge.
pnpm dev
```

Open http://127.0.0.1:5173. The scene, movement, ordering, events, and ending work without provider keys. Live dialogue can use an Anthropic key or the free Horde configuration below; the default online Edge voices need no speech billing key. Missing dialogue credentials produce an explicit message. Keys remain on the Node server. For a hosted Cloudflare deployment, set `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` as a server/container secret; never put it in the browser bundle or a `VITE_` variable.

For the hosted game, add `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` as a secret binding on the `table-for-two` Worker; the allowlisted binding is forwarded to Node. See [Cloudflare deployment and secret setup](deployment/cloudflare/README.md). A configuration change restarts the backend, so existing in-memory evenings expire and players start another evening. The current deployment uses one `basic` managed Node container with a ten-minute idle timeout. Cloudflare hosting and Anthropic usage are separate from the free speech path.

The restaurant prepares its shaders while the menus remain usable. A brief Loading restaurant status clears after the first completed draw; movement and E interaction then become available.

For a production build, run `pnpm build` followed by `pnpm start`. Use HTTPS when hosting beyond localhost. Both bundled servers set the COOP/COEP headers required by local recognition. A reverse proxy must preserve those headers and allow streaming responses without buffering.

## Play

- Play opens the three-mode menu. Solo date opens character selection, Double date opens the invite room, and Blind date starts a surprise solo evening. Arrows choose a menu item; Enter confirms. Game menus support arrows, Enter, Tab and Escape, while native settings controls retain their usual keys.
- WASD / arrows: walk. Click the scene to capture the mouse; drag-look is available when pointer lock is unavailable.
- Mouse: look. E: use the object at the center of the view. R or a table-prop click: inspect; E or Use acts on the inspected object, and Back/Escape returns to your date.
- Walk to your table and look at the chair to sit. Look down at the menu to order, or at your glass to drink. Table actions still offers a toast or raising your glass.
- T opens typed conversation, M toggles the microphone, and J opens the journal. These actions are also available from Pause. Escape closes typed input and retains the draft; sending closes the field. Voice turns do not overwrite a typed draft.
- G opens or closes Table actions and releases the mouse without pausing. Menu, glass, course choices, and table games live in this drawer; new interruptions open it automatically.
- Escape closes the active input or menu first; outside these surfaces it pauses dinner and microphone capture. You can finish or leave from the pause menu.
- Settings are available from the title and Pause: voice playback, captions, ambience and volume. The model picker has been removed. Normal play defaults to Haiku; developer settings only adjust spending limits and show provider usage when enabled.

Recognition runs locally in a worker using Moonshine TinyStreaming. First use downloads roughly 49 MB of model data plus a 13 MB WASM runtime, then caches it. A loading message is displayed; typed conversation remains usable if the model cannot load. No microphone audio is sent to a transcription API. In Double date, enabling the mic also opts into peer-to-peer friend voice. Local recognition and friend voice lease independent tracks from one physical capture; turning the mic off releases both. A disconnected friend must opt in again after reconnecting.

## AI, voices, and budgets

Each completed player turn makes one conversation-provider request. In solo and blind modes it directs the date plus at most one spoken waiter/diner reaction. In Double date it directs both independent dates and the supporting cast, with at most two short spoken lines total; the AI can leave space for friend banter with no spoken response. Shared scene state, recent dialogue, delivery receipts, and compact memory inform responses. There are no background cast calls, automatic model retries or separate summarization requests.

Partial microphone transcripts can produce transient local listening expressions. Tentative reactions revise when the wording changes and clear on a final turn, pause, target change or expiry. They never generate dialogue, captions, model requests or conversation memory, and do not add prompt tokens. Actual spoken replies still wait for the completed turn. Consequential emotions, interruptions and farewells take priority over the preview.

New dates default to Anthropic Haiku unless the server is configured for entirely free Horde dialogue. The optional [Dolphin Mistral 24B Venice model through OpenRouter](https://openrouter.ai/cognitivecomputations/dolphin-mistral-24b-venice-edition) requires the server-only `OPENROUTER_API_KEY`. It stays locked until that couple earns confidence and two distinct reciprocal flirting exchanges have actually been delivered across separate turns. After unlocking, a local transcript check routes adult sexual-topic discussion to the available alternative; ordinary conversation and non-graphic flirting stay on Haiku. Short follow-ups inherit the recent topic, while a concrete topic change returns to Haiku. No classifier model or extra inference is used. Each friend earns automatic access independently. Score claims, raw model requests, host-wide switching, new-room settings and chat commands cannot skip the Dolphin gate. Friendship, accepted boundary disregard and departure remove automatic access. Without an alternative provider, Haiku continues normally.

Dolphin uses JSON mode rather than strict JSON-schema enforcement. The full contract is sent as instructions and the same server validators still own speakers, actions, memory, boundaries and scoring. Its listed text rates are $0.20 per million input and $0.90 per million output tokens; cached input is conservatively estimated at the ordinary input rate. At 60 turns of 4,000 input and 100 output tokens this is approximately $0.0534, excluding purchase fees; actual structured replies and context vary. There is no free hosted guarantee or live Dolphin quality benchmark. HTTP cancellation is implemented, but upstream billing stopping immediately on cancellation has not been verified. No Dolphin inference was purchased during preparation.

The roleplay context identifies fictional adult characters, preserves consent and provider rules, and returns underage framing to ordinary conversation. On the Anthropic route, flirting stays non-graphic and explicit detail moves to a cutaway. Provider refusals are distinct from NPC boundaries: a known content-limit response may cause a brief waiter check or an eligible real wrong-order incident, without another model request, pressure penalty or automatic ending. Ordinary outages still appear as errors. A personal refusal must be specific and actually delivered to that human; only subsequent exact, same-topic repeated disregard can justify a departure. Topic changes, unclear recognition and another listener’s missed warning cannot manufacture strikes. New dialogue direction addresses the latest human intent before scenery; reusable greetings and incident clips are offered only while they fit the current scene.

`DATE_SPEECH_PROVIDER=edge` is the temporary default for free online character speech. It uses the pinned MIT [node-edge-tts client](https://github.com/SchneeHertz/node-edge-tts) and stable Microsoft neural voices for each character, configurable with `DATE_EDGE_VOICE_*`. Spoken text is sent to Microsoft's consumer speech service; this is separate from the local Moonshine recognizer, which sends no microphone audio to a transcription API. Edge can change, throttle or become unavailable without a service guarantee.

The voices use small rate/pitch adjustments for delivery. Edge does not offer dependable ElevenLabs-style emotional acting through this integration; [upstream documents the custom-style limitation](https://github.com/rany2/edge-tts/issues/426). Five selected voice IDs returned real MP3 audio in short free-service checks, but those samples are not a release voice audition or an end-to-end game latency benchmark.

If Edge fails before playback, the browser can use an available English device voice and keep that choice stable per character. A successful device fallback avoids repeated server waits for 30 seconds, then probes Edge again. Device voices vary by browser and installed voice packs; blocked or unavailable speech leaves captions usable. Interrupting a line stops either playback path and records only its actual delivery. No waveform amplitude is invented for device speech.

ElevenLabs remains available only through an explicit `DATE_SPEECH_PROVIDER=elevenlabs` setting plus its API key. Its `DATE_VOICE_*`, model and cost settings apply only in that mode. An existing ElevenLabs key does not change the free default, and an Edge outage never switches to a paid provider. `DATE_SPEECH_PROVIDER=none` disables server synthesis for caption-only configurations and offline test fixtures.

`DATE_SESSION_LIMIT_USD` defaults to $2 per date and is the server ceiling for the developer setting. `DATE_DAILY_LIMIT_USD` defaults to $10 per UTC day. Reservations prevent overlapping paid requests from bypassing these limits; an atomic ledger in server/restaurant/runtime/budget.json preserves the daily budget across restarts. An unfinished reservation is counted conservatively after recovery. Configure provider-side spending limits as an additional account-level ceiling. Costs shown in developer settings are estimates from returned token counts and speech usage. Edge and device speech add $0 in speech API charges; generated server characters and cache hits are still counted. Paid ElevenLabs submissions can still be billed when canceled.

The default conversation model is `claude-haiku-5-5`. There is no visible model picker. Private typed controls `/model haiku`, `/model sonnet`, and `/model horde` set the requested model for subsequent dialogue; `/model` reports the selection. An explicit choice persists on this device, while old picker-saved choices are ignored. A Haiku request remains eligible for automatic adult-topic routing after chemistry is earned; explicit Sonnet or Horde selection stays on that route until changed or reset. `/model reset` clears that saved override, cancels pending dialogue and speech, and restores the server's normal model routing while preserving the current evening, memory and progress. Reset remains available while a typed reply is waiting; in Double it also drops queued turns and clears a conversation error. Horde selection works immediately without a chemistry unlock. Double-date switches and resets remain host-only. Control text never enters dialogue, captions, shared room lines or model requests, and switching itself makes no inference request. Production enables private choices with `DATE_ALLOW_MODEL_SELECTION=1`, which also makes free anonymous Horde available. Optional `DATE_HAIKU_MODEL` / `DATE_SONNET_MODEL` values configure upstream model aliases; update pricing if changing them.

AI Horde uses community-hosted models and charges no model API fees. Its native transport looks up trusted, mature-content-capable workers before the first turn, caches availability briefly, and selects the estimated fastest compatible model pool. It submits exactly one job with the final player message; status polling does not start extra generations. Worker lookup can happen early, but Horde cannot reserve a blank queue position and accept revised dialogue later. Jobs are canceled on interruption or timeout, with no automatic inference retries or paid fallback. Full speaker, action, boundary, memory and agenda validation remains in force. Replies are capped at 512 tokens; model availability, format quality and waiting time vary. Volunteer hosts may inspect or retain submitted messages, so this is not a private inference service.

Set `DATE_CONVERSATION_PROVIDER=aihorde` for an entirely free conversation configuration from the start; this mode excludes paid models and always stays on Horde. Alternatively, `DATE_EARNED_PROVIDER=aihorde` keeps automatic Haiku-first play while using Horde for adult-topic turns after chemistry is earned. A model-selection-enabled server also uses Horde for those turns when Dolphin is not configured. Unlocking never permanently switches ordinary dialogue away from Haiku; `/model horde` remains an immediate deliberate override, and `/model reset` restores automatic routing. `AI_HORDE_TRANSPORT=native` uses the larger worker pool; the chat transport is available as an explicit server configuration. A blank `AI_HORDE_API_KEY` uses the public anonymous account. A registered Horde account can improve queue priority. Leave `AI_HORDE_MODEL` blank for automatic selection from the reviewed compatible pool, or pin a supported model. `AI_HORDE_TIMEOUT_MS` defaults to 60 seconds, with a 120-second maximum. The Cloudflare bridge forwards these settings and allows for the same wait budget. Existing Edge voice remains independent of the conversation provider.

The conversation policy permits non-graphic discussion of sex, compatibility and boundaries, plus bold reciprocal flirting, teasing and innuendo. Mentioning a mature topic alone is not pressure or misconduct. Characters retain their own preferences and boundaries; the policy does not ask for graphic sexual acts or vulgar detail.

Provider status confirms whether a key is configured, rather than proving a successful model call. API credit, model-access and rejected request-setting errors are reported distinctly without returning raw provider error bodies or credentials.

The configured standard API rates were checked on November 8, 2026 against the official [Haiku 5.5](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/models/haiku-5-5/overview) and [Sonnet 5.5](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/models/sonnet-5-5/overview) pages. Per million tokens, Haiku's up-to-100k prompt tier is $0.10 input/$0.50 output, and Sonnet is $2/$10; cache-write/read rates are included in accounting. Game and evaluation contexts are bounded below Haiku's higher-priced over-100k prompt tier. This is estimated accounting, not a provider invoice or a promise about any subscription's included credits.

The existing ElevenLabs clip generator is an optional **paid utility**, separate from the free runtime default:

```powershell
pnpm generate:clips             # report clips and estimated cost, without generation
pnpm generate:clips --generate  # explicitly pay to synthesize using ElevenLabs
```

The generator loads `.env`, reuses complete existing clips, and respects `DATE_CLIP_LIMIT_USD` (default $2). Leave it in dry-run mode for the free setup. It writes `server/restaurant/clips/manifest.json` and MP3s. Restart the server after generating. Runtime audio is cached by exact text, provider, voice, emotion, speech model, prosody and output settings. Cache hits make no synthesis request. Legacy ElevenLabs clips are not substituted for an Edge voice. No pregenerated audio is fabricated or included by default.

For identical Haiku/Sonnet evaluation scenes, run `pnpm compare:models` for a dry run. `pnpm compare:models --run` makes the paid text-only calls and writes `.qa/date-model-comparison.json` with responses, tokens, costs, and latency. The default run cap is $0.50 (`DATE_EVAL_LIMIT_USD`). The five cases cover humor, cast reactions, boundaries, memory, and interrupted dialogue. Quality scores remain unrated until a person reviews them. The evaluation command has its own run cap; it is separate from the restaurant server's daily ledger.

## Architecture and verification

- `src/restaurant`: separate content, types, furniture layout, first-person rendering, DOM UI, local recognition, and speech playback.
- Rendering separates replaceable character rigs, prop slots, botanical geometry and materials. Bundled CC0 walnut/plaster/cloth maps are documented in [environment asset credits](public/environment-assets/README.md), and the small plant/vase GLB kit in [prop credits](public/restaurant-props/README.md). All assets load locally and share GPU resources. The exterior uses authored low-poly buildings and subtle glass rain, with a static reduced-motion mode; the archived Seoul photo is no longer rendered. Tripo remains deferred.
- Character rigs have distinct adult proportions, shaped garments and continuously skinned trousers, with layered strand-textured hair. Mira wears oxblood leather with a swept ponytail; Jules has a dark bob and relaxed ink suit; Dev has a short side part and petrol knit polo. Facial performance includes eyelids, brows, gaze and speaking lips. Cast materials share one small reflection probe, and replacement characters retain the same resource ownership and deterministic seating paths.
- `server/restaurant`: authoritative Rapier scene/controller, authenticated HTTP sessions, validated streamed conversation turns, Edge and ElevenLabs adapters, audio cache, and usage accounting.
- `server/double-date`: private per-member room authentication, authoritative four-actor physics, shared conversation scheduling, voice signaling, and independent pair outcomes. Gameplay snapshots and signaling use bounded HTTP polling; speech uses a separate streaming response. A generated line has one server-side producer shared by both listeners, while playback receipts remain per listener.
- Double and solo sessions use the same provider cache and persistent account budget ledger. No separate double-date budget can bypass the daily limit.
- Remote friend voice accepts `DATE_ICE_SERVERS_JSON` (or `VOICE_ICE_SERVERS_JSON`) with standard RTC ICE server definitions. The default public STUN server is useful for direct connectivity; configure a TURN service for networks that require relay. Local two-browser testing does not establish internet/NAT reliability.
- Session state and conversation memory live in server RAM. A reload restores the same session with a browser-session token; a server restart clears it. No cross-session relationship memory or microphone recordings are stored.
