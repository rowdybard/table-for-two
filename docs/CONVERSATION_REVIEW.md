# Human conversation review

The corpus and evaluation runner are implemented. **No real baseline/candidate outputs or completed human A/B review exist as part of this change.** Injected-provider checks establish execution, protocol validation, cost guards and bookkeeping. They do not establish that anyone preferred the dialogue or found it funny, flirty or memorable. All report ratings and baseline-comparison status begin **unrated**.

The corpus contains 60 individually authored eight-turn scripts: 12 each for Mira, Jules and Dev, 12 seeded Blind dates, and 12 attributed Double dates. Six additional 30-turn fixtures test corrections, ownership, notebook retention and an offstage pairing. Scripts and human-review checkpoints live in `src/restaurant/evaluation-fixtures.ts`. The runner uses production system, turn-note, transcript, persona-sheet and validation builders plus the production social and delivery controllers. It uses controlled dining snapshots; it does not run physics, course timers, speech recognition, synthesis, a browser or the complete transport.

7. **Compute the approved gates from real review rows.** Candidate preference is candidate wins divided by baseline wins plus candidate wins, excluding ties; report both reviewers' counts, pooled non-tied counts, ties and capture coverage. Report continuity failures and serious invented/unheard/cross-couple facts separately; averages cannot erase them. Publish numerator, denominator, excluded/missing cases and actual quoted evidence. These are review gates, not a guarantee that a clip or game will go viral.

## Offline review packets

After approved matched captures exist, these commands read local reports only. They do not call a provider, run the game or assign scores:

```sh
pnpm review:conversations prepare --baseline baseline.json --candidate candidate.json --packets .qa/review/human --key .qa/review-private/key.json
pnpm review:conversations gate --baseline baseline.json --candidate candidate.json --key .qa/review-private/key.json --reviews completed-1.json,completed-2.json
```

Give each reviewer only their `reviewer-N.json` and the generated `cast-reference.json`. Keep the coordinator key private until review is complete. Preparation counterbalances A/B order, anonymizes cast names, preserves missing/failed turns and per-listener delivery markers, and leaves all judgments and ten rubric scores null. Existing artifacts are never overwritten. The gate verifies report hashes, immutable provenance, matched settings and unmodified transcripts. It requires two independent human attestations and complete coverage; missing ratings or capture gaps stay blocked. Output separates each reviewer's preference counts from pooled metrics and enforces the documented thresholds without an AI judge. A passing text review still does not verify audio performance or authorize deployment.
