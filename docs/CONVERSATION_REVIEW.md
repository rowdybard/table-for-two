## Offline review packets

After approved matched captures exist, these commands read local reports only. They do not call a provider, run the game or assign scores:

```sh
pnpm review:conversations prepare --baseline baseline.json --candidate candidate.json --packets .qa/review/human --key .qa/review-private/key.json
pnpm review:conversations gate --baseline baseline.json --candidate candidate.json --key .qa/review-private/key.json --reviews completed-1.json,completed-2.json
```

Give each reviewer only their `reviewer-N.json` and the generated `cast-reference.json`. Keep the coordinator key private until review is complete. Preparation counterbalances A/B order, anonymizes cast names, preserves missing/failed turns and per-listener delivery markers, and leaves all judgments and ten rubric scores null. Existing artifacts are never overwritten. The gate verifies report hashes, immutable provenance, matched settings and unmodified transcripts. It requires two independent human attestations and complete coverage; missing ratings or capture gaps stay blocked. Output separates each reviewer's preference counts from pooled metrics and enforces the documented thresholds without an AI judge. A passing text review still does not verify audio performance or authorize deployment.
