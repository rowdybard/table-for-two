The owner wants believable, funny, flirty dates with distinct people and earned callbacks. Deepen the existing game rather than add features.

Horde commands are removed.
- **Delivery/performance:** bounded streams, requests, acknowledgments and voice waits recover without a new evening; late audio cannot replay. Delivered utterances refresh restrained reactions; listening/speech outrank incidental gestures. Low-tier rendering is cheaper.
