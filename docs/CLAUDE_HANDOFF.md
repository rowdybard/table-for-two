## Direction and changes already live

The owner wants believable, funny, flirty dates with distinct people and earned callbacks. Deepen the existing game rather than add features.

- **Table only:** player starts seated, date arrives second, conversation unlocks at the chair. Walking, waiter/diner speech, menus/service chores, forced scenarios, agenda objectives and canned interruptions are inactive. Keep Solo, seeded Blind and Double; do not restore removed choreography.

- **Natural conversation:** confident flirting, specific teasing, disagreement, ordinary replies and occasional stories are permitted. Every reply need not be a joke, trust examination or question. At most two relevant, untold seeds per turn; additional context stays within the 600-token estimate.

Disclosures commit only after the date's own listener heard/read them. Covered captions do not count as read. - **Earned attraction/routing:** interest heart is presentation only; spending/chores never buy romance. Default is Haiku; `/model dolphin` uses dolphinllm.com, `/model reset` restores normal routing without resetting the date. Earned chemistry does not force every later turn onto Dolphin. Horde commands are removed.
- **Delivery/performance:** bounded streams, requests, acknowledgments and voice waits recover without a new evening; late audio cannot replay. Delivered utterances refresh restrained reactions; listening/speech outrank incidental gestures. Low-tier rendering is cheaper. Inactive pages stop drawing/polling, release mic/recognition/audio/voice connections, abort pending work, and defer WebGL/resize; return preserves the evening with mic off.

A lost or corrupt ledger must be recovered by hand, never recreated at zero.
